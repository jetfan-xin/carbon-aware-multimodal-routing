# Public operational-data gap audit

Public sources partially narrow five of six requested evidence gaps but fully close none. They support historical Luchaogang rail connectivity, published water frequency/throughput, port-process commitments, a transfer-performance boundary, observed procurement payload ranges and default mode factors. They do not disclose a complete operational dataset.

Remaining high-priority gaps are:

- current Chongqing--Shanghai rail origin, timetable, frequency and cancellation distribution;
- whether the CNY 4,600 rail comparison includes Shanghai drayage;
- Luchaogang--Yangshan contract prices, completed orders and waiting-time distribution;
- water booking wait, on-time reliability and operator-specific quotes;
- shipment-level OD, weight, cargo, release and deadline records;
- measured equipment/load-factor emissions for specific trucks, trains and vessels.

Therefore public throughput totals cannot be converted into shipment rows, and a timetable mention cannot be converted into available booking capacity. The machine-readable record is [`operational_evidence_gaps.json`](../data/operational_evidence_gaps.json).
