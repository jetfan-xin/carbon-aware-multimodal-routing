# Facility network evidence inventory

This directory is generated by `python3 -B tools/build_facility_network.py`.
It is an evidence inventory, not a completed routing benchmark.

- `facilities.csv` contains 20 facility candidates, their resolution level,
  cargo role, admission decision and status in the historical and current
  snapshots.
- `services.csv` contains twelve published service records and states the
  fields still missing before each record can become an optimizer edge.
- `transfers.csv` contains five documented intermodal capabilities and their
  missing handling parameters.
- `summary.json` contains machine-readable counts.
- `facility-network.svg` is a schematic. Dashed links mean published service
  evidence; they do not mean that a complete numeric edge has been inferred.

The current inventory deliberately has zero optimizer-eligible service edges
and zero optimizer-eligible transfers. Public sources confirm operations but
do not yet provide a consistent tuple of exact endpoints, distance, service
time, tariff and handling data. The end-to-end corridor calibration remains a
separate experiment until those gaps are filled.

`summary.json` also reports seven calibrated model edges and three runnable
cases from the separate `model_inputs.json` layer. Those counts do not change
the raw evidence status: field-level assumptions are retained and analyzed in
[`../facility-case-analysis`](../facility-case-analysis/README.md).
